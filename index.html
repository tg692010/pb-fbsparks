Inside the FBISD SPARKS STEM Center

FACILITY HIGHLIGHT

Modern Architectural Landmark with Sustainable Design

Featuring a signature curved timber accent facade, high-efficiency solar roof panel array, wide glass entrance plaza, and an outdoor testing courtyard for mobile robotics and drone testing.

  • Facility Size: 15,000+ Square Feet

Multi-Level Glass Team Bays & Practice Field

Individual glass-walled team bays (*PowerBreakers 22045*, *Titan Robotics*, *Cyber Sharks*, *Code Warriors*) surrounding a full regulation FTC practice field, complete with upper-level spectator balconies and precision tool suites.

THE PROBLEM VS. THE SPARKS VISION

Why FBISD Needs a Central Facility Now

While neighboring Texas school districts invest in multi-million dollar innovation centers, FBISD teams disassemble heavy equipment daily for cafeteria lunch.

❌ THE CURRENT REALITY

Cramped Hallways & Equity Gaps

  • ✕
    Building in Cafeterias & Storage Closets

    Teams lose 45 minutes every afternoon setting up and tearing down makeshift workspaces before and after school.

  • ✕
    Zero Access to Heavy Precision Machinery

    No CNC routers, metal lathes, or waterjet cutters on any FBISD campus, forcing teams to rely on expensive private vendors.

  • ✕
    Improvised Practice Fields

    Robots are calibrated on uneven hallway carpets, leading to field alignment errors during official competitions.

  • ✕
    District Socio-Economic Equity Disparities

    Underfunded campuses without wealthy booster clubs cannot afford off-site rental space, leaving talented students behind.

✅ PROPOSED FBISD SPARKS CENTER

State-of-the-Art Central Hub

  • ✓
    15,000 Sq Ft Centralized Innovation Complex

    A dedicated facility housing modular team bays, secure tool storage, fiber internet, and collaborative design zones.

  • ✓
    Industrial Precision Machining Lab

    Equipped with CNC mills, laser cutters, 3D printing suites, and welding stations supervised by certified technical staff.

  • ✓
    500-Seat Competition Arena & Bleachers

    Year-round access to regulation practice fields plus a spectator stadium with double Jumbotron scoreboards.

  • ✓
    District-Wide STEM Equity for All 11 High Schools

    Equal access for every high school, middle school feeder program, and CTE student across Fort Bend ISD.
